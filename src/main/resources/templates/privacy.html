Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of data required to manage clan operations:

  • Discord Information: User ID, Username, and Avatar (provided via Discord OAuth2).
  • Clan Data: In-game Player Name, Power, and Team compositions related to Siege/HvH.
  • Logs: We log login activity and critical actions (Audit Log) for security purposes.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used solely for:

  • Authenticating your access to the site.
  • Managing Clan Siege strategies and Roster coordination.
  • Displaying clan statistics and history.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. Data is visible only to verified clan members and administrators.

4. Cookies

We use a single session cookie (JSESSIONID) to keep you logged in. This is essential for the site to function. This cookie is valid for 1 year after logging in. After that the website will require you to log in again.
